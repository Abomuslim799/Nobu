# Speaking: hikoya aytish
meta: Dushanba · zamonlar bilan · 10 daq

## 🟢 Asos
Hikoya = **fon → voqea → natija**. Fon — past continuous, voqealar — past simple, oldingi voqea — past perfect.
EN: It was raining and I **was waiting** for the bus. Suddenly a car **stopped**. The driver **had seen** me before…

Tayyor qolip:
- **Boshlash**: So, the other day… / Last summer… / You won't believe what happened…
- **Davom**: Then… / After that… / All of a sudden…
- **Tugatish**: In the end… / So that's how… / Looking back, I…

## 🟡 O'rta
**1-2-3 qoidasi**: 1 daqiqa, 2 ta tafsilot (rang, his, ovoz), 3 ta bog'lovchi so'z.
- His qo'shing: I was **so nervous**. I **couldn't believe** my eyes.
- Ishlatish uchun ifodalar: **It turned out that…** (ma'lum bo'ldiki), **To make matters worse…**, **Luckily / Unfortunately…**

## 🔴 Advanced
- Jonli hikoya — present tense: So I **walk** in, and there **he is**…
- Hikoya ichida fikr: **I remember thinking** "this is a disaster".
- Kulminatsiya oldidan pauza va intonatsiya ko'tarilishi.
- Inversiya bilan: **No sooner had I** left **than** it started raining.

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- 5 ta tayyor hikoyangiz bo'lsin (sayohat, ish, bolalik, xato, quvonch). Har haftada bir xil hikoyani **yangi grammatika** bilan qayta ayting.
- Yozib oling (ovoz yozish), tinglang, 1 ta xatoni toping — mistake bank'ga.

## ✅ Bugungi topshiriq
- "Tell me about a time you got lost" — 1 daqiqa, telefonga yozib oling.
- Kamida: 1 ta past continuous, 1 ta past perfect, 1 ta **It turned out that**.
