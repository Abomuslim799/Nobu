# Articles: a / an / the / —
meta: Murphy EGU 69–81 · A2 → C1

## 🟢 Asos
| Artikl | Qachon | Misol |
|---|---|---|
| **a / an** | bitta, sanaladigan, birinchi marta, "qandaydir" | I saw **a** dog. |
| **the** | ikkalamiz biladigan, aniq, yagona | **The** dog was huge. **The** sun. |
| **—** (yo'q) | ko'plik yoki sanalmaydigan, umuman | **Dogs** are loyal. I like **music**. |

**a** vs **an** — talaffuz bo'yicha: **an** hour, **an** MBA, **a** university, **a** European.

## 🟡 O'rta
- Kasb: She's **a** doctor. (~~She's doctor~~)
- Umumiy gap — ko'plik/sanalmaydigan artiklsiz: **Life** is short. **Water** is important. Aniq bo'lsa — the: **The water** in this bottle…
- **the** bilan: daryo, okean, tog' tizmasi, ko'plikdagi davlatlar (**the** Nile, **the** USA, **the** UK), cholg'ular (play **the** piano), superlativ (**the** best), tartib son (**the** first).
- **artiklsiz**: shaharlar, ko'pchilik davlatlar, ko'llar, alohida tog'lar, ovqatlar (have **breakfast**), sport (play **football**), tillar, **go to work / school / bed / hospital** (vazifasi bo'yicha).
- **at home, by car, on foot, next week, last year** — artiklsiz.

## 🔴 Advanced
- **the + sifat** = guruh: **the** rich, **the** elderly, **the** unemployed.
- **the + birlik** = tur (rasmiy): **The** tiger is endangered.
- go to **school** (o'quvchi sifatida) ↔ go to **the** school (ota-ona yig'ilishiga).
- **a** ma'nosi "har biri": twice **a** week, $10 **an** hour.
- Ma'no farqi: **a** few / few, **a** little / little (ijobiy / salbiy).

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- Tinglovchi "**qaysi?**" deb so'rashi mumkinmi? Mumkin → **a**. Biladi → **the**.
- Sanaladigan birlik ot **hech qachon yalang'och** turmaydi: a/the/my/this kerak.
- Umumlashtiryapsizmi (barcha itlar, umuman musiqa)? → **artiklsiz**.

## ❌ Tipik xatolar
- ~~I am engineer~~ → I'm **an** engineer
- ~~The life is beautiful~~ → **Life** is beautiful
- ~~I play the football~~ → I play **football**
- ~~She is best student~~ → **the** best student

## ✅ O'zingni tekshir
- "U Toshkentdagi eng yaxshi kafe" → It's **the** best café in Tashkent.
- "Men haftada uch marta yuguraman" → I run three times **a** week.
