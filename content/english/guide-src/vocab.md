# So'z va collocation: qanday o'rganish
meta: Har kuni · 30 so'z + 10 collocation · ~25 daq

## 🟢 Asos
Ilovadagi **So'zlar** bo'limi: yangi so'zlar (kvota) + takror. Kartani bosing → javob ochiladi → **Hard / Normal / Easy** ni o'zingiz tanlang.
- **Hard** — bilmadim yoki ikkilandim → tez-tez qaytadi
- **Normal** — esladim, lekin o'yladim
- **Easy** — darhol bildim → kam qaytadi (30 kundan keyin yana tekshiriladi)

> 💡 O'zingizni aldamang: 2 soniyadan ortiq o'ylasangiz — Easy emas.

## 🟡 O'rta
**Collocation** — so'zlar juftligi. Alohida so'zdan muhimroq, chunki tayyor bo'laklar bilan gapirasiz.
| ❌ O'zbekchadan tarjima | ✅ Collocation |
|---|---|
| do a mistake | **make** a mistake |
| make homework | **do** homework |
| strong rain | **heavy** rain |
| say a lie | **tell** a lie |
| take a decision (AmE kam) | **make** a decision |

Asosiy fe'llar: **make, do, take, have, get, give, keep, pay, catch, break** — har biri bilan 10 ta collocation.

## 🔴 Advanced
- **Phrasal verbs** — ma'no bo'yicha guruhlang: look into (tekshirmoq), come up with (o'ylab topmoq), put off (kechiktirmoq).
- **Sinonimlar nozikligi**: big / large / huge / enormous; look / see / watch / stare / glance.
- **Register**: get → obtain (rasmiy), buy → purchase, help → assist.

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- Har yangi so'zni **gap ichida** o'rganing, alohida emas: ~~ambitious = shuhratparast~~ → *She's very **ambitious** — she wants to be CEO.*
- Kuniga 3 ta yangi so'zni **o'sha kuni** speaking'da ishlating.
- Hard so'zlar ro'yxatini kechqurun 1 marta ovoz chiqarib o'qing.

## ✅ Bugungi topshiriq
- So'zlar: yangi kvota + takror (ilova ko'rsatadi).
- Bugungi 3 ta so'z bilan 3 ta gap tuzib ayting.
