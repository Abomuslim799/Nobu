# Speaking: qayta aytish (retell)
meta: Juma · reported speech · 10 daq

## 🟢 Asos
Video, maqola yoki suhbatni **o'z so'zlaringiz bilan** aytish. Paraphrase — C1 ning asosiy ko'nikmasi.
- **He said that…**, **She told me…**, **According to…**, **The main idea was that…**

## 🟡 O'rta
Reporting verbs bilan jonli:
- He **admitted** making a mistake.
- She **suggested** trying again.
- They **refused** to comment.
- He **warned** us not to…

## 🔴 Advanced
- Xulosa: **In a nutshell…**, **The gist of it is…**, **What he was getting at was…**
- Masofa: **Apparently…**, **Allegedly…**, **He is said to have…**
- O'z fikrini qo'shish: **I'm not sure I agree with him, though.**

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- **3 ta kalit so'z** yozing (boshqa hech narsa) va 1 daqiqa qayta ayting. Matnga qaramang.

## ✅ Bugungi topshiriq
- 3–5 daqiqalik inglizcha video ko'ring, 1 daqiqada qayta ayting.
- Kamida: 3 xil reporting verb.
