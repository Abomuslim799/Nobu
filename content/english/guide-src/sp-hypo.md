# Speaking: xayoliy vaziyatlar
meta: Payshanba · shart gaplar · 10 daq

## 🟢 Asos
"What would you do if…?" — IELTS va suhbatlarda eng ko'p savol.
EN: If I **won** the lottery, I'**d** travel first, then I'**d** probably invest.

Javob qolipi: **javob → sabab → misol → shart**.

## 🟡 O'rta
- O'tmish afsusi: If I **had known**…, I **would have**… / I **wish I had**…
- Aralash: If I **had studied** harder, I'**d be** in a better job **now**.
- Vaqt yutish: **That's a tough one…**, **I've never really thought about it, but…**

## 🔴 Advanced
- **Were I to…**, **Had it not been for…**, **Suppose…**, **Imagine if…**
- **Assuming that…**, **Provided that…**, **Otherwise…**

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- Savolni o'zingizga qaytaring: "If I could live anywhere? Hmm… I'd probably choose…" — 3 soniya vaqt yutasiz.

## ✅ Bugungi topshiriq
- "If you could change one decision in your life, what would it be?" — 1.5 daqiqa.
- Kamida: 3-shart, aralash shart, 1 ta wish.
