# Talaffuz: so'z urg'usi
meta: Shanba · 5 daq

## 🟢 Asos
Noto'g'ri urg'u — so'zni tanib bo'lmaydi. Urg'uli bo'g'in **balandroq, uzunroq, aniqroq**.
| Qoida | Misollar |
|---|---|
| 2 bo'g'inli ot/sifat — 1-bo'g'in | **TA**ble, **HAP**py, **WIN**dow |
| 2 bo'g'inli fe'l — 2-bo'g'in | be**GIN**, de**CIDE**, re**LAX** |
| -tion, -sion, -ic, -ical — oldingi bo'g'in | infor**MA**tion, eco**NOM**ic |
| -ity, -ogy, -graphy — 3-bo'g'in oxirdan | elec**TRI**city, pho**TOG**raphy |

## 🟡 O'rta
Ot ↔ fe'l juftliklari:
- **RE**cord (ot) — re**CORD** (fe'l)
- **PRE**sent — pre**SENT**
- **OB**ject — ob**JECT**
- **IN**crease — in**CREASE**

## 🔴 Advanced
- Gap urg'usi (sentence stress): yangi ma'lumot urg'u oladi: I didn't say **HE** stole it (boshqasi aytdi).
- Murakkab otlar — 1-qism: **GREEN**house, **BLACK**board.

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- Lug'atda ˈ belgisi — urg'u. Yangi so'zni o'rganganda urg'usini ham yodlang.
- Tez-tez xato qilinadigan: **com**fortable, **ve**getable, de**ve**lop, **ho**tel → ho**TEL**, **ca**reer → ca**REER**.

## ✅ Bugungi topshiriq
- photograph — photography — photographic: urg'u ko'chishini 5 marta ayting.
