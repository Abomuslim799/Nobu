# Reported speech
meta: Murphy EGU 47–48 · B1 → C1

## 🟢 Asos
O'tgan zamondagi say/tell dan keyin zamon **bir qadam orqaga**:
| To'g'ri gap | Ko'chirma |
|---|---|
| "I **am** tired." | He said he **was** tired. |
| "I **will** come." | He said he **would** come. |
| "I **have seen** it." | He said he **had seen** it. |
| "I **saw** it." | He said he **had seen** it. |
| "I **can** swim." | He said he **could** swim. |

**say** vs **tell**: **say** (something) — He **said** that…; **tell** + **kimga** — He **told me** that…

## 🟡 O'rta
- **Savol**: oddiy tartib, so'roq belgisisiz. She asked **where I lived** (~~where did I live~~). Yes/no → **if / whether**: He asked **if I spoke** English.
- **Buyruq / iltimos**: told / asked + **to V1**: She **told me to close** the door. **not to**: He told me **not to be** late.
- Vaqt va joy so'zlari: today → that day, tomorrow → the next day, here → there, this → that.
- Fakt hali ham to'g'ri bo'lsa, orqaga surish shart emas: He said he **lives** in Tashkent.

## 🔴 Advanced
**Reporting verbs** — tuzilmasi bilan:
| Fe'l | Tuzilma | Misol |
|---|---|---|
| admit / deny | + -ing | He **denied taking** the money. |
| suggest | + -ing / that | She **suggested going** out. |
| refuse / promise / agree / offer | + to V1 | He **refused to help**. |
| warn / advise / remind / encourage | + kim + to V1 | She **warned me not to go**. |
| apologise / insist | + for/on + -ing | He **apologised for being** late. |

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- **tell** dan keyin doim **kim**: told **me**, told **him**. say dan keyin — yo'q (said **to** me).
- Savolni ko'chirishda: savol so'zi + **ega + fe'l** (oddiy gapdagidek).

## ❌ Tipik xatolar
- ~~He said me~~ → He **told** me / He **said to** me
- ~~She asked where did I live~~ → She asked where **I lived**
- ~~He suggested to go~~ → He suggested **going**

## ✅ O'zingni tekshir
- "U charchaganini aytdi" → He **said** he **was** tired.
- "U menga kechikmaslikni aytdi" → She **told me not to be** late.
