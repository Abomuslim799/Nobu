# may / might / could — taxmin
meta: Murphy EGU 29–30 · B1 → C1

## 🟢 Asos
Ishonch darajasi bo'yicha:
| Ishonch | Tasdiq | Inkor |
|---|---|---|
| ~95% | He **must** be at home. | He **can't** be at home. |
| ~70% | He **should** be at home. | — |
| ~50% | He **may / might / could** be at home. | He **may not / might not** be at home. |

> ⚠️ Taxmin inkorida **couldn't / can't** = imkonsiz; **might not** = balki yo'q. ~~mustn't~~ taxmin uchun ishlatilmaydi.

## 🟡 O'rta
- Hozir davom etayotgan haqida: He **might be sleeping**. She **must be working**.
- Kelajak: It **might rain** later. We **may move** next year.
- **could** taxmin: That **could** be true. (could not ≠ might not — couldn't = imkonsiz)

## 🔴 Advanced
- O'tmish taxmini — **modal + have V3**: He **must have** forgotten. They **might have** got lost. She **can't have** seen us.
- **may well / might well** — ehtimoli yuqori: Prices **may well** rise.
- **might as well** — boshqa yaxshi variant yo'q: We **might as well** walk.

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- Dalil bor (chiroq yonib turibdi) → **must**. Dalil yo'q, shunchaki ehtimol → **might**.
- Taxminning inkori: **can't** (aniq yo'q), **might not** (balki yo'q).

## ❌ Tipik xatolar
- ~~He mustn't be at home~~ (taxmin) → He **can't** be at home
- ~~Maybe he might come~~ (ortiqcha) → He **might** come / **Maybe** he'll come
- ~~He must to be~~ → He must **be**

## ✅ O'zingni tekshir
- "U charchagan bo'lsa kerak — tun bo'yi ishladi" → She **must be** tired.
- "Bu rost bo'lishi mumkin emas" → It **can't be** true.
