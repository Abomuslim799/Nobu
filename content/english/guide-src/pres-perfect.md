# Present perfect simple / continuous
meta: Murphy EGU 7–12 · A2 → C1

## 🟢 Asos
Present perfect — o'tmish va **hozir** o'rtasidagi ko'prik: natija hozir muhim yoki davr hali tugamagan.

| | Simple | Continuous |
|---|---|---|
| Tasdiq | I **have finished** · she **has finished** | I **have been working** |
| Inkor | I **haven't finished** | I **haven't been working** |
| Savol | **Have** you **finished**? | **Have** you **been working**? |

EN: I'**ve lost** my keys. || hozir yo'q — natija
EN: I'**ve never been** to London. || hayotdagi tajriba
EN: I'**ve been waiting** for an hour. || hali davom etyapti

## 🟡 O'rta
**Simple vs continuous:**
| Simple — natija, nechta | Continuous — jarayon, qancha vaqt |
|---|---|
| I'**ve written** 3 emails. | I'**ve been writing** emails all morning. |
| I'**ve read** 100 pages. | I'**ve been reading** for two hours. |
| Someone **has drunk** my tea! (bo'sh) | Someone **has been drinking** my tea! (yarmi qolgan) |

**for / since:**
- **for** + davomiylik: for 5 years, for two hours
- **since** + boshlanish nuqtasi: since 2020, since Monday, since I was a child

So'zlar: **just** (hozirgina), **already** (allaqachon), **yet** (hali — inkor/savol), **ever** (hech qachon — savol), **never**, **so far**, **recently**.

> 💡 State verb'lar faqat simple: I'**ve known** him for years (~~have been knowing~~ emas).

## 🔴 Advanced
- **It's the first / second time + present perfect**: It's the first time I'**ve flown**.
- **Superlative + present perfect**: It's the best film I'**ve ever seen**.
- Yaqinda tugagan, izi qolgan harakat — continuous: Why are you wet? — I'**ve been running**.
- **How long have you…?** savoliga javob — for/since; **When did you…?** — past simple.
- British vs American: BrE *I've just eaten*, AmE ko'pincha *I just ate*.

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- Savol ber: **"Hozirga ta'siri bormi?"** Bor → perfect.
- **for / since / how long** → perfect continuous (state verb bo'lsa simple).
- Raqam yoki natija (**3 ta, 100 sahifa, tugadi**) → simple; vaqt davomiyligi (**2 soat, butun kun**) → continuous.
- Aniq o'tgan vaqt (**yesterday, ago, in 2019**) bo'lsa — perfect **emas**, past simple.

## ❌ Tipik xatolar
- ~~I am living here for 5 years~~ → I'**ve been living** here for 5 years
- ~~I have seen him yesterday~~ → I **saw** him yesterday
- ~~I know him since 2010~~ → I'**ve known** him since 2010
- ~~Did you ever been…?~~ → **Have** you ever **been**…?

## ✅ O'zingni tekshir
- "Ertalabdan beri seni kutyapman" → I'**ve been waiting** for you since the morning.
- "Hali qaror qilmadim" → I **haven't decided** yet.
- "Bu yerda 5 yildan beri ishlayman" → I'**ve worked** / I'**ve been working** here for 5 years.
