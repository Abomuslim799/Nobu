# Past perfect simple / continuous
meta: Murphy EGU 15–16 · B1 → C1

## 🟢 Asos
Past perfect — **o'tmishdagi o'tmish**: o'tmishdagi bir nuqtadan OLDIN bo'lgan voqea.
| | Simple | Continuous |
|---|---|---|
| Tasdiq | I **had finished** | I **had been working** |
| Inkor | I **hadn't finished** | I **hadn't been working** |
| Savol | **Had** you **finished**? | **Had** you **been working**? |

EN: When I arrived, the film **had** already **started**. || avval film boshlandi, keyin men keldim
EN: She was tired because she **had been working** all day. || davomiylik o'sha nuqtagacha

## 🟡 O'rta
- **by the time, before, after, already, just, never… before** bilan ko'p keladi.
- Simple — natija; continuous — o'sha nuqtagacha davom etgan jarayon (ko'pincha ko'rinadigan iz bilan): The ground was wet — it **had been raining**.
- Reported speech'da: He said he **had seen** it. (He said: "I saw it")
- Ketma-ketlik aniq bo'lsa (after, before) past simple ham bo'ladi: After I **finished**, I left.

## 🔴 Advanced
- 3-shart: If I **had known**, I would have come.
- wish + past perfect: I wish I **had studied** harder.
- Inversiya: **Had** I **known**… / **No sooner had** I sat down **than**… / **Hardly had**… **when**…
- Hikoyada "orqaga qaytish" (flashback): She looked at the house. She **had lived** there for twenty years.

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- O'tmishda **ikki voqea** bo'lsa, birinchisiga **had + V3** qo'ying.
- O'zbekcha "**-gan edi**" ko'pincha past perfect.
- Vaqt chizig'i: ●(had done) ——— ●(past simple) ——— NOW

## ❌ Tipik xatolar
- ~~When I arrived, the film started~~ (birga boshlandi degani) → the film **had started**
- ~~I had went~~ → I **had gone**
- Keraksiz ishlatish: ~~I had visited London last year~~ (bitta voqea) → I **visited** London last year

## ✅ O'zingni tekshir
- "U kelguncha biz ovqatlanib bo'lgan edik" → By the time he arrived, we **had eaten**.
- "Ko'zlari qizil edi — yig'lagan ekan" → Her eyes were red; she **had been crying**.
