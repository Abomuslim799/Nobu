# Future continuous / perfect
meta: Murphy EGU 24–25 · B1 → C1

## 🟢 Asos
| Shakl | Ma'no | Misol |
|---|---|---|
| will **be -ing** | kelajakdagi lahzada davom etayotgan | This time tomorrow I'**ll be flying**. |
| will **have V3** | kelajakdagi nuqtagacha tugagan | By Friday I'**ll have finished**. |
| will **have been -ing** | o'sha nuqtagacha davomiylik | By June I'**ll have been working** here for 5 years. |

## 🟡 O'rta
- **by + vaqt** (by Friday, by the time…) → future perfect.
- **this time tomorrow / next week**, **at 8 tomorrow** → future continuous.
- Muloyim so'rov (rejani so'rash): **Will you be using** the car tonight? (bosimsiz)
- **by the time + present**: By the time you **arrive**, we'**ll have left**.

## 🔴 Advanced
- Taxmin (hozir haqida): Don't call him now — he'**ll be sleeping**. / He'**ll have arrived** by now.
- Rasmiy: The project **will have been completed** by May. (passive)

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- Kelajakdagi **nuqtada nima qilayotgan bo'lasiz** → be -ing. O'sha **nuqtagacha nima qilib bo'lasiz** → have V3.
- **by** ko'rdingizmi — future perfect deyarli kafolat.

## ❌ Tipik xatolar
- ~~By Friday I will finish~~ (ma'no: juma kuni tugataman) → I'**ll have finished**
- ~~By the time you will arrive~~ → By the time you **arrive**

## ✅ O'zingni tekshir
- "Soat 9 da qo'ng'iroq qilma — ovqatlanayotgan bo'laman" → I'**ll be having** dinner.
- "Yil oxirigacha 1000 so'z o'rganib bo'laman" → I'**ll have learned** 1000 words by the end of the year.
