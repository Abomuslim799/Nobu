# Talaffuz: -ed va -s qo'shimchalari
meta: Juma · 5 daq

## 🟢 Asos
**-ed** uch xil aytiladi:
| Tovush | Qachon | Misollar |
|---|---|---|
| /t/ | jarangsiz undoshdan keyin (p, k, s, sh, ch, f) | work**ed**, stopp**ed**, watch**ed** |
| /d/ | jarangli va unlidan keyin | play**ed**, liv**ed**, call**ed** |
| /ɪd/ | **t** yoki **d** dan keyin | want**ed**, need**ed**, decid**ed** |

> ⚠️ ~~work-ED~~, ~~play-ED~~ — xato. /ɪd/ faqat t/d dan keyin.

## 🟡 O'rta
**-s / -es** ham uch xil:
| Tovush | Qachon | Misollar |
|---|---|---|
| /s/ | jarangsizdan keyin | book**s**, work**s** |
| /z/ | jarangli va unlidan keyin | dog**s**, play**s** |
| /ɪz/ | s, z, sh, ch, j dan keyin | bus**es**, watch**es**, page**s** |

## 🔴 Advanced
- Undosh to'plami: **asked** /ɑːskt/, **months** /mʌnθs/, **texts** /teksts/ — tovush tashlamang.
- Linking bilan: walk**ed i**n → walk-tin.

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- Qo'lni bo'g'izga qo'ying: titrasa — jarangli (/d/, /z/), titramasa — jarangsiz (/t/, /s/).

## ✅ Bugungi topshiriq
- worked, played, wanted, asked, decided, watched — ovoz yozib, tekshiring.
