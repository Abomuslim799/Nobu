# Present simple / continuous
meta: Murphy EGU 1–4 · A2 → C1

## 🟢 Asos
**Present simple** — doimiy narsalar: odat, fakt, jadval. **Present continuous** — hozir yoki shu kunlarda davom etayotgan, vaqtinchalik narsa.

| | Present simple | Present continuous |
|---|---|---|
| Tasdiq | I **work** · she **works** | I **am working** · she **is working** |
| Inkor | I **don't work** · she **doesn't work** | I'**m not working** · she **isn't working** |
| Savol | **Do** you **work**? · **Does** she **work**? | **Are** you **working**? · **Is** she **working**? |

EN: I **drink** coffee every morning. || odat
EN: I'**m drinking** coffee right now. || hozir
EN: Water **boils** at 100°C. || fakt
EN: The train **leaves** at 7:30. || jadval

> 💡 he / she / it — fe'lga **-s**: work**s**, go**es**, stud**ies**, ha**s**.

## 🟡 O'rta
Continuous yana uch holatda ishlatiladi:
- **vaqtinchalik holat**: I **live** in Tashkent (doimiy) ↔ I'**m living** with my parents this month (vaqtinchalik)
- **o'zgarish, rivojlanish**: Prices **are rising**. My English **is getting** better.
- **kelishilgan reja**: I'**m meeting** Ali tomorrow at 6.

**State verbs** — holat fe'llari continuous'da ishlatilmaydi: know, understand, believe, mean, want, need, like, love, hate, prefer, belong, own, seem, remember.
EN: I **understand** you. || ~~I am understanding~~ emas

Ba'zi fe'llar ikki ma'noli: holat (simple) yoki harakat (continuous).
| Fe'l | Holat — simple | Harakat — continuous |
|---|---|---|
| think | I **think** you're right (fikr) | I'**m thinking** about it (o'ylayapman) |
| have | I **have** a car (egalik) | I'**m having** lunch (ovqatlanyapman) |
| see | I **see** what you mean (tushunaman) | I'**m seeing** a doctor today (uchrashaman) |
| taste / smell | The soup **smells** good | The chef **is smelling** the soup |

## 🔴 Advanced
- **always + continuous** — shikoyat, jahl: She'**s always losing** her keys!
- **be + being + sifat** — vaqtinchalik xulq: You'**re being** silly. (odatda bunday emassan)
- Hikoya va sport sharhlarida present tense jonlilik beradi: So I **walk** into the room, and he**'s sitting** there…
- Rasmiy uslubda state verb'lar continuous'da muloyimlik beradi: I'**m hoping** you can help. We'**re wondering** if…

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- Savol ber: **"Hozirmi yoki doimmi?"** Hozir/vaqtinchalik → continuous; doim/odat → simple.
- Gapda **now, at the moment, look!, listen!, these days** bo'lsa → deyarli doim continuous.
- **always, usually, often, never, every day** → simple (shikoyatdan tashqari).
- Fe'l "his, fikr, egalik, xohish" bo'lsa → simple. "Men buni hozir **qilyapman**mi?" deb so'rang: qilib bo'lmasa (bilish, yoqtirish) — state verb.

## ❌ Tipik xatolar
- ~~I am understand~~ → I **understand**
- ~~She don't like~~ → She **doesn't** like
- ~~Are you agree?~~ → **Do** you agree?
- ~~I am living here since 2020~~ → I'**ve been living** here since 2020 (since/for → perfect)
- ~~What you are doing?~~ → What **are you** doing?

## ✅ O'zingni tekshir
- "Men seni tushunmayapman" → I **don't understand** you.
- "U shu hafta onasinikida turibdi" → She'**s staying** with her mother this week.
- "Bu sumka kimniki?" → Who **does** this bag **belong** to?
- "Narxlar oshib boryapti" → Prices **are going up**.
