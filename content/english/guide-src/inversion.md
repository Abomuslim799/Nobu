# Inversion — teskari tartib
meta: AGU · B2 → C1

## 🟢 Asos
Inkor yoki cheklovchi so'z gap boshiga chiqsa, **savoldagi kabi tartib** (yordamchi fe'l + ega) keladi. Bu — rasmiy va kuchli urg'u.
| Oddiy | Inversiya |
|---|---|
| I have **never** seen such a mess. | **Never have I** seen such a mess. |
| I **rarely** go out. | **Rarely do I** go out. |
| I had **hardly** arrived when… | **Hardly had I** arrived when… |

## 🟡 O'rta
Eng ko'p ishlatiladiganlar:
- **Never / Rarely / Seldom** + do/have: **Seldom do we** see such talent.
- **Not only… but also**: **Not only is he** smart, **but** he's **also** kind.
- **No sooner… than / Hardly… when**: **No sooner had I** sat down **than** the phone rang.
- **Only then / Only after / Only when**: **Only then did I** understand.
- **Under no circumstances / In no way**: **Under no circumstances should you** open it.
- **Little** (bilmaslik): **Little did he know** that…

## 🔴 Advanced
- Shart inversiyalari: **Had I known** (if I had known), **Were I** you, **Should you** need…
- **Not until… did**: **Not until** I left **did I** realise.
- **So / Such** + inversiya: **So tired was I** that I fell asleep.
- Joy inversiyasi (yordamchi fe'lsiz): Here **comes the bus**. On the hill **stood a castle**.

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- Formula: **Inkor so'z + yordamchi fe'l (do/does/did/have/had/can) + ega + asosiy fe'l**. Xuddi savol tuzgandek.
- Gapirishda 3 tasini o'zlashtiring: **Not only…**, **Never have I…**, **Little did I know…** — C1 taassurot beradi.

## ❌ Tipik xatolar
- ~~Never I have seen~~ → Never **have I** seen
- ~~Not only he is smart~~ → Not only **is he** smart
- ~~Only then I understood~~ → Only then **did I** understand

## ✅ O'zingni tekshir
- "Bunday go'zal joyni hech ko'rmaganman" → **Never have I seen** such a beautiful place.
- "U nima bo'lishini bilmas edi" → **Little did he know** what would happen.
