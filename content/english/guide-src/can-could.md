# can / could / be able to
meta: Murphy EGU 26 · A2 → B2

## 🟢 Asos
| Shakl | Ma'no | Misol |
|---|---|---|
| **can / can't** | hozirgi qobiliyat, ruxsat, imkoniyat | I **can** swim. You **can't** park here. |
| **could** | o'tmishdagi umumiy qobiliyat; muloyim so'rov | I **could** read at 5. **Could** you help me? |
| **be able to** | boshqa zamonlar | I'**ll be able to**… I'**ve never been able to**… |

## 🟡 O'rta
**O'tmishdagi BIR MARTALIK uddalash** — could emas, **was able to / managed to**:
EN: The fire was big, but everyone **was able to** escape. || ~~could escape~~ emas
EN: I **couldn't** open the door. || inkorda could mumkin

- Sezgi fe'llari bilan can: I **can** hear music. I **could** see the sea.
- Ruxsat so'rash: **Can** I…? (oddiy) → **Could** I…? → **May** I…? (rasmiy)

## 🔴 Advanced
- **could have V3** — imkoniyat bor edi, ishlatilmadi: You **could have** told me!
- **can't have V3** — o'tmish haqida inkor taxmin: He **can't have** seen us.
- **can** umumiy imkoniyat: It **can** get very cold here in winter.

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- O'tmish + "**uddaladim**" (bir voqea) → **managed to**. O'tmish + "**qila olardim**" (umuman) → **could**.
- Kelajakda **will can** yo'q → **will be able to**.

## ❌ Tipik xatolar
- ~~I will can~~ → I'**ll be able to**
- ~~I could pass the exam yesterday~~ → I **managed to** / **was able to** pass
- ~~Can you to help?~~ → Can you **help**?

## ✅ O'zingni tekshir
- "Kecha muammoni hal qila oldim" → I **managed to** solve the problem yesterday.
- "Kelasi yil erkin gapira olaman" → I'**ll be able to** speak fluently next year.
