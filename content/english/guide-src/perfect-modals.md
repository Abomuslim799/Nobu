# Perfect modals: should / would / could / must have
meta: Murphy EGU 27–28 + AGU · B2 → C1

## 🟢 Asos
**modal + have + V3** — o'tmish haqida.
| Shakl | Ma'no | Misol |
|---|---|---|
| **should have** | kerak edi (qilmading) — afsus/tanqid | You **should have** told me. |
| **shouldn't have** | kerak emas edi (qilding) | I **shouldn't have** said that. |
| **could have** | imkoniyat bor edi | We **could have** won. |
| **would have** | shart bajarilganda bo'lardi | I **would have** helped (if I'd known). |
| **must have** | aniq taxmin | He **must have** forgotten. |
| **might / may have** | balki | They **might have** got lost. |
| **can't / couldn't have** | imkonsiz | She **can't have** done it. |

## 🟡 O'rta
- Talaffuzda qisqaradi: should've /ʃʊdəv/, could've, would've, must've. Yozishda ~~should of~~ — xato!
- Continuous: He **must have been sleeping** (o'sha paytda).
- **needn't have** — qilding, kerak emas edi: You **needn't have** waited.

## 🔴 Advanced
- **could have** tanqid: You **could have** at least called!
- **would have liked to** — afsus: I **would have liked to** see it.
- Passive: The letter **must have been sent** to the wrong address.
- **was to have done** (rasmiy, amalga oshmagan reja): He **was to have arrived** yesterday.

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- O'tmish haqida gapirsangiz va modal kerak bo'lsa — **har doim have + V3** qo'shing. Modal o'zi o'tmish shaklga ega emas.
- O'zbekcha "**…ishim kerak edi**" → should have; "**…gan bo'lsa kerak**" → must have; "**…ishi mumkin edi**" → could have.

## ❌ Tipik xatolar
- ~~You should told me~~ → You should **have told** me
- ~~He must forgot~~ → He must **have forgotten**
- ~~should of~~ → should **have**

## ✅ O'zingni tekshir
- "Men ko'proq o'qishim kerak edi" → I **should have studied** more.
- "U poyezdga ulgurmagan bo'lsa kerak" → He **must have missed** the train.
- "Biz yutishimiz mumkin edi" → We **could have won**.
