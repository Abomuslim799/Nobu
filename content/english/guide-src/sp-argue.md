meta: Yakshanba · hedging + inversion · 15 daq

## 🟢 Asos
**PREP** formulasi: **Point → Reason → Example → Point**.
EN: I think remote work is better. **The main reason is** time — no commute. **For example**, I save two hours a day. **That's why** I prefer it.
