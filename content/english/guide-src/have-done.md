# have something done / it is said
meta: Murphy EGU 45–46 · B1 → C1

## 🟢 Asos
**have / get + narsa + V3** — ishni boshqa odamga qildirish:
EN: I **had my hair cut**. || sartaroshda
EN: I need to **get my car repaired**.
Noxush hodisa: I **had my phone stolen**.

## 🟡 O'rta
**It is said / believed / thought / expected that + gap**:
EN: **It is said that** he is very rich.
**Kimdir + is said / believed + to V1 / to have V3**:
EN: He **is said to be** very rich.
EN: The thief **is believed to have escaped**. || o'tmish — to have V3

| Gap | Ma'no |
|---|---|
| It is said that he lives in Paris. | Aytishlaricha, u Parijda yashaydi |
| He is said to live in Paris. | (xuddi shu) |
| He is said to have lived in Paris. | Aytishlaricha, u Parijda yashagan |

## 🔴 Advanced
- **have someone do** (buyurish): I **had** the mechanic **check** the brakes.
- **get someone to do**: I **got** him **to help** me.
- Yangiliklar uslubi: *The minister **is reported to have resigned**.*

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- O'zingiz qilmadingizmi? Pul to'lab qildirdingizmi? → **had + narsa + V3**.
- "**Aytishlaricha / deb hisoblanadi**" → is said / is believed / is thought.

## ❌ Tipik xatolar
- ~~I cut my hair~~ (sartaroshda) → I **had my hair cut**
- ~~I had cut my hair~~ (tartib) → I had **my hair cut**
- ~~He is said that he is rich~~ → He is said **to be** rich / **It** is said that…

## ✅ O'zingni tekshir
- "Mashinamni ta'mirlatishim kerak" → I need to **have my car repaired**.
- "U eng yaxshi o'yinchi deb hisoblanadi" → He **is considered (to be)** the best player.
