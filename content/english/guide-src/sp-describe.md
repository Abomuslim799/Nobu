# Speaking: tasvirlash
meta: Shanba · relative clauses · 10 daq

## 🟢 Asos
Odam, joy, narsani tasvirlash: **umumiy → tafsilot → his**.
EN: It's a small café **which** is hidden behind the market. The owner, **who** is really friendly, makes the best coffee **I've ever had**.

## 🟡 O'rta
- Sifatlar zanjiri (tartib): **opinion → size → age → colour → origin** (a lovely small old red Italian car).
- Taqqoslash: It's **much bigger than**…, **not as… as**, **the most… I've ever**.
- Noaniq: **kind of**, **sort of**, **-ish** (greenish, thirtyish).

## 🔴 Advanced
- Reduced relatives: a man **wearing** a hat, a house **built** in 1900.
- Kuchli sifatlar: huge, tiny, stunning, filthy, freezing, packed.
- Taassurot: **It gives off a… vibe**, **It reminds me of…**, **What strikes you first is…**

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- "good, nice, bad" — **taqiq**. O'rniga aniq so'z: delicious, cosy, chaotic.

## ✅ Bugungi topshiriq
- Sevimli joyingizni 1 daqiqa tasvirlang.
- Kamida: 2 ta who/which, 1 ta reduced clause, 3 ta kuchli sifat.
