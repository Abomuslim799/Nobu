# wish / if only / I'd rather / it's time
meta: Murphy EGU 41 + AGU · B2 → C1

## 🟢 Asos
| Tuzilma | Ma'no | Misol |
|---|---|---|
| wish + **past** | hozirgi holatdan norozilik | I wish I **had** more time. |
| wish + **had V3** | o'tmishdagi afsus | I wish I **hadn't said** that. |
| wish + **would** | boshqaning xulqidan norozilik | I wish you **would** stop. |
| wish + **could** | qobiliyat | I wish I **could** fly. |

**If only** — xuddi wish, lekin kuchliroq his: **If only** I **had** listened!

## 🟡 O'rta
- **be** — were (rasmiy), was (gapirishda): I wish I **were** taller.
- **wish + would** o'zingiz haqida emas: ~~I wish I would~~ → I wish I **could** / **did**.
- **I'd rather + V1** (o'zim): I'**d rather stay** home.
- **I'd rather + kimdir + past**: I'**d rather you didn't** smoke here.
- **It's (high) time + past** / **to V1**: It's time we **left**. It's time **to go**.

## 🔴 Advanced
- **would rather + have V3**: I'**d rather have stayed** home.
- **Suppose / Supposing + past**: Suppose you **lost** your job?
- **as if / as though + past** (xayoliy): He talks **as if** he **knew** everything.

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- wish'dan keyin har doim **bir qadam orqaga**: hozir → past, o'tmish → past perfect.
- Boshqadan shikoyat → **would**; o'zingizning qobiliyatingiz → **could**.

## ❌ Tipik xatolar
- ~~I wish I have~~ → I wish I **had**
- ~~I wish I didn't say that~~ (o'tmish) → I wish I **hadn't said**
- ~~It's time we go~~ → It's time we **went** / It's time **to go**

## ✅ O'zingni tekshir
- "Qani endi ko'proq vaqtim bo'lsa" → I wish I **had** more time.
- "Qani endi o'sha gapni aytmagan bo'lsam" → I wish I **hadn't said** that.
