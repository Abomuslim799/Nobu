# Talaffuz: intonatsiya
meta: Yakshanba · 5 daq

## 🟢 Asos
| Ohang | Qachon | Misol |
|---|---|---|
| ↘ pasayuvchi | tasdiq, wh-savol, buyruq | I live in Tashkent↘. Where do you live↘? |
| ↗ ko'tariluvchi | yes/no savol, ishonchsizlik | Are you ready↗? |
| ↘↗ tushib-ko'tariluvchi | ikkilanish, "lekin" | Well↘↗… maybe. |

## 🟡 O'rta
- Ro'yxat: ↗ ↗ ↗ ↘ — I bought apples↗, bread↗ and milk↘.
- Question tag: ↘ (bilaman, tasdiqlang) / ↗ (rostdan bilmayman).
- Muloyimlik: so'rovlarda ohang ko'tarilsa — yumshoqroq: Could you help me↗?

## 🔴 Advanced
- Ajablanish: **Really↗?** / **You did↗?**
- Qarama-qarshilik: I like **tea**↘, but **coffee**↗… not so much.
- Bir tekis ohang = befarqlik, qo'pollik. Rus/o'zbek talaffuzidagi tekis ohangdan qoching.

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- **Shadowing**: aktyor gapini 1 soniya kechikib, ohangini aynan takrorlang. Ma'noga emas, **musiqaga** e'tibor bering.

## ✅ Bugungi topshiriq
- 30 soniyalik film parchasini 5 marta shadowing qiling.
