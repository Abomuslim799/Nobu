# used to / would / be used to
meta: Murphy EGU 18, 61 · B1 → C1

## 🟢 Asos
**used to + V1** — o'tmishda bor edi, hozir yo'q (odat yoki holat).
EN: I **used to smoke**. || hozir chekmayman
EN: There **used to be** a cinema here.
| Tasdiq | Inkor | Savol |
|---|---|---|
| I **used to** live there | I **didn't use to** live there | **Did** you **use to** live there? |

## 🟡 O'rta
**would** — o'tmishdagi takroriy **harakat** (hikoyada, nostalgiya). Holat uchun emas.
EN: Every summer we **would go** to the mountains. || ✓ harakat
EN: I **used to have** long hair. || ✓ (~~I would have long hair~~ — holat)

**be used to + -ing / ot** — ko'nikkan; **get used to** — ko'nikib borish:
EN: I'**m used to getting** up early.
EN: You'll soon **get used to** the noise.

| Tuzilma | Ma'no | Keyin |
|---|---|---|
| used to | oldin shunday edi | V1 |
| be used to | ko'nikkanman | -ing / ot |
| get used to | ko'nikyapman | -ing / ot |

## 🔴 Advanced
- **would** hikoyada birinchi gapdan keyin used to o'rnini egallaydi: When I was a kid, I **used to** spend summers at my gran's. She **would** wake me at six…
- **will** — hozirgi odat (ba'zan jahl bilan): He **will** leave his socks everywhere!
- **be accustomed to + -ing** — rasmiy sinonim.

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- **to** dan keyin -ing kelsa (be/get used **to** + -ing) — bu yerdagi "to" predlog. Tekshiruv: o'rniga ot qo'yib bo'ladimi? (used to **the noise**) — bo'lsa, -ing.
- Holat fe'li (be, have, live, know) bo'lsa → **used to**, would emas.

## ❌ Tipik xatolar
- ~~I'm used to wake up early~~ → I'm used to **waking** up early
- ~~I didn't used to~~ → I didn't **use** to
- ~~I use to play~~ (hozirgi) → I usually play

## ✅ O'zingni tekshir
- "Bolaligimda Samarqandda yashar edim" → I **used to live** in Samarkand as a child.
- "Hali bu shaharga ko'nikmadim" → I'**m not used to** this city yet.
