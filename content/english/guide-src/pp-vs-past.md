# Present perfect vs Past simple
meta: Murphy EGU 13–14 · B1 → C1

## 🟢 Asos
Asosiy savol — **vaqt aniqmi va tugaganmi?**
| Past simple | Present perfect |
|---|---|
| aniq o'tgan vaqt: yesterday, last week, in 2019, ago | vaqt aytilmagan yoki davr tugamagan |
| I **saw** this film **yesterday**. | I'**ve seen** this film. |
| I **lost** my phone **last week**. | I'**ve lost** my phone. (hozir yo'q) |

## 🟡 O'rta
- **When…?** — doim past simple: When **did** you **arrive**? (~~When have you arrived~~)
- **today, this week, this year** — davr tugamagan bo'lsa perfect: I'**ve worked** a lot today. Tugagan bo'lsa past: I **drank** coffee this morning (hozir kechqurun).
- Yangilik perfect bilan boshlanadi, tafsilot past bilan davom etadi: I'**ve had** an accident! — Oh no, what **happened**? — I **crashed** into a tree.
- Vafot etgan yoki tugagan holat — past: My grandfather **worked** in Russia for 10 years (hozir yo'q).

## 🔴 Advanced
- Gazeta va yangiliklar: sarlavha perfect, matn past: *Prices **have risen** sharply. They **rose** by 5% in May.*
- **since** gapida: perfect + since + past: I'**ve known** him since we **were** at school.
- Rasmiy hisobotda: *The company **has grown** steadily since 2015.* (trend hozirgacha)

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- Gapda **vaqt so'zi bormi?** Aniq o'tgan vaqt bo'lsa — **past**. Yo'q yoki **ever / never / yet / already / just / so far / since / for** bo'lsa — **perfect**.
- O'zbekcha "-gan**man**" (tajriba) → perfect; "-di" + vaqt → past.

## ❌ Tipik xatolar
- ~~I have finished it yesterday~~ → I **finished** it yesterday
- ~~When have you seen him?~~ → When **did** you **see** him?
- ~~I didn't see him since Monday~~ → I **haven't seen** him since Monday

## ✅ O'zingni tekshir
- "Londonda ikki marta bo'lganman" → I'**ve been** to London twice.
- "Men 2019-yilda Londonga bordim" → I **went** to London in 2019.
- "Bu uyga qachon ko'chib kelding?" → When **did** you **move** into this house?
