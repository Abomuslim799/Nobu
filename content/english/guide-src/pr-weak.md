# Talaffuz: kuchsiz shakllar va schwa /ə/
meta: Chorshanba · 5 daq

## 🟢 Asos
Inglizchada eng ko'p tovush — **schwa /ə/** (bo'sh, urg'usiz "ə"). Kichik so'zlar gapda **qisqarib** ketadi.
| So'z | Kuchli | Kuchsiz (gapda) |
|---|---|---|
| to | /tuː/ | /tə/ — I want **to** go |
| for | /fɔː/ | /fə/ — It's **for** you |
| and | /ænd/ | /ən/ — fish **and** chips |
| can | /kæn/ | /kən/ — I **can** swim |
| of | /ɒv/ | /əv/ — a cup **of** tea |
| was | /wɒz/ | /wəz/ |

## 🟡 O'rta
- Modal + have: **should have** → /ʃʊdəv/, **could have** → /kʊdəv/.
- **can** (tasdiq, kuchsiz /kən/) vs **can't** (kuchli /kɑːnt/) — farq unli uzunligida.
- Ko'p bo'g'inli so'zlarda: **a**bout /əˈbaʊt/, doct**or** /ˈdɒktə/, Lond**on** /ˈlʌndən/.

## 🔴 Advanced
- **gonna, wanna, gotta, kinda** — norasmiy nutqda tinglash uchun bilish shart.
- Gap oxirida kuchli shakl: What are you looking **at**? /æt/

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- Faqat **muhim so'zlarni** (ot, fe'l, sifat) aniq ayting, qolganini "yutib" yuboring — shunda ritm tabiiy bo'ladi.

## ✅ Bugungi topshiriq
- "I want to go to the shop for a bottle of water" — kuchsiz shakllar bilan 5 marta.
