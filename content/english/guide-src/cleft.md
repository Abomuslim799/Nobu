# Cleft sentences + emphasis
meta: AGU · B2 → C1

## 🟢 Asos
Cleft — gapni ikkiga bo'lib, bir qismini **ta'kidlash**.
| Oddiy | It-cleft | What-cleft |
|---|---|---|
| Ali broke the window. | **It was Ali who** broke the window. | — |
| I need a holiday. | — | **What I need is** a holiday. |
| I hate his attitude. | **It's his attitude (that)** I hate. | **What I hate is** his attitude. |

## 🟡 O'rta
- **What + ega + fe'l + is/was**: **What surprised me was** his calm.
- **All (that) + ega + fe'l + is**: **All I want is** some sleep. (faqat shu)
- **The thing / reason / place / person + …is**: **The reason I'm late is** the traffic. **The place where we met was** a café.
- Harakatni ta'kidlash: **What I did was** (to) call the police. **What happened was**…

## 🔴 Advanced
- **do / does / did** bilan urg'u (tasdiqda): I **do** like it! She **did** call, I promise.
- **It wasn't until… that**: **It wasn't until** midnight **that** he arrived.
- **the very, -self, at all, whatsoever** kuchaytiruvchilar: I have no idea **whatsoever**. He told me **himself**.
- Fronting: **This** I can't accept. **Strange** it may seem, but…

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- Bahs-munozarada **What I mean is…**, **The point is…**, **What really matters is…** — fikrni tartiblash uchun tayyor ochilishlar.
- O'zbekchadagi "**aynan**" ko'pincha it-cleft: Aynan Ali oynani sindirdi → It was Ali who…

## ❌ Tipik xatolar
- ~~What I need it is a holiday~~ → What I need **is** a holiday
- ~~It was Ali which broke~~ → **who** broke
- ~~The reason is because~~ → The reason is **that**

## ✅ O'zingni tekshir
- "Menga faqat bir oz uyqu kerak" → **All I need is** some sleep.
- "U yarim tungacha kelmadi" → **It wasn't until midnight that** he arrived.
