# Talaffuz: v va w
meta: Seshanba · 5 daq

## 🟢 Asos
| Tovush | Qanday | Misollar |
|---|---|---|
| /v/ | yuqori tishlar pastki labga tegadi | **v**ery, **v**oice, lo**v**e |
| /w/ | lablar yumaloq, tishlar tegmaydi (o'zbekcha "u" ga yaqin) | **w**ater, **w**ork, **w**hy |

> ⚠️ O'zbeklar ko'pincha ikkalasini bir xil "v" deydi: ~~vork~~ (work), ~~vater~~ (water).

## 🟡 O'rta
Minimal pairs:
- vest — west
- vine — wine
- verse — worse
- very well — /v/ + /w/

## 🔴 Advanced
- **w** yozilib aytilmaydi: **w**rite, **w**rong, ans**w**er, t**w**o, s**w**ord.
- **wh** — odatda /w/: what, where, why; lekin **who, whole** → /h/.

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- /w/ uchun: "u" deb boshlang, keyin tez unliga o'ting: u-ork → work.
- Barmoqni pastki labga qo'ying — /w/ da lab tishga tegmasligi kerak.
