# Indirect questions + question tags
meta: Murphy EGU 49–52 · B1 → C1

## 🟢 Asos
**Indirect savol** — muloyimroq. Kirish qismidan keyin **oddiy tartib**:
| To'g'ri savol | Indirect |
|---|---|
| Where **is** the station? | Could you tell me where **the station is**? |
| What time **is it**? | Do you know what time **it is**? |
| **Does** he live here? | I wonder **if he lives** here. |

Kirishlar: Could you tell me…? Do you know…? I wonder… I'm not sure… Can you remember…?

## 🟡 O'rta
**Question tags** — "shundaymi?":
- tasdiq → **inkor** tag: You're coming, **aren't you**?
- inkor → **tasdiq** tag: She isn't here, **is she**?
- yordamchi fe'l takrorlanadi; yo'q bo'lsa do/does/did: You live here, **don't you**? He went, **didn't he**?

| Gap | Tag |
|---|---|
| I'm right | **aren't I**? |
| Let's go | **shall we**? |
| Close the door (buyruq) | **will you**? |
| Nobody called | **did they**? |
| There's a bank here | **isn't there**? |

## 🔴 Advanced
- Intonatsiya: ↘ tasdiq kutyapman (bilaman), ↗ haqiqiy savol (bilmayman).
- **Same-way tag** (qiziqish/ajablanish): So you're leaving, **are you**?
- Rasmiy indirect: **I was wondering if** you could…

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- Indirect savolda **do/does/did yo'qoladi** va ega fe'ldan oldin keladi — xuddi oddiy gap kabi.
- Tag: gapdagi yordamchi fe'lni topib, ishorasini teskari qiling.

## ❌ Tipik xatolar
- ~~Do you know where is the station?~~ → where **the station is**
- ~~Can you tell me what time does it start?~~ → what time **it starts**
- ~~I'm right, am not I?~~ → **aren't I**?

## ✅ O'zingni tekshir
- "Bu qancha turishini ayta olasizmi?" → Could you tell me how much **this costs**?
- "U haydashni biladi, shundaymi?" → He can drive, **can't he**?
