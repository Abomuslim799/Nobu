# Past simple / continuous
meta: Murphy EGU 5–6 · A2 → C1

## 🟢 Asos
**Past simple** — o'tmishda tugagan voqea. **Past continuous** — o'tmishdagi bir lahzada davom etayotgan harakat (fon).

| | Past simple | Past continuous |
|---|---|---|
| Tasdiq | I **worked** · I **went** | I **was working** · they **were working** |
| Inkor | I **didn't work** | I **wasn't working** |
| Savol | **Did** you **work**? | **Were** you **working**? |

EN: I **visited** London in 2019. || tugagan
EN: At 9 pm I **was watching** TV. || o'sha lahzada davom etayotgan

> 💡 Noto'g'ri fe'llarni (go–went, see–saw, buy–bought…) 100 tasini yodlang — gapirishda eng ko'p kerak bo'ladigan ro'yxat.

## 🟡 O'rta
**Uzun fon + qisqa voqea** — eng muhim qolip:
EN: I **was cooking** when you **called**. || pishirish — fon, qo'ng'iroq — voqea
EN: While we **were eating**, the lights **went** out.

- **when** + qisqa voqea (past simple); **while / as** + uzun harakat (continuous)
- Ketma-ket voqealar — hammasi simple: I **opened** the door, **went** in and **turned** on the light.
- Ikki parallel uzun harakat — ikkalasi continuous: While I **was working**, he **was sleeping**.
- Hikoyada fon tasviri: The sun **was shining**, birds **were singing**…

## 🔴 Advanced
- **was going to** — amalga oshmagan reja: I **was going to** call you, but I forgot.
- **was / were + -ing** muloyim so'rov: I **was wondering** if you could help me.
- **used to / would** — o'tmish odati (alohida mavzu), past simple ham bo'ladi: I **played** football every day as a child.
- Ma'no farqi: When he arrived, we **had dinner** (u kelgach boshladik) ↔ we **were having** dinner (u kelganda ovqatlanayotgan edik) ↔ we **had had** dinner (allaqachon tugatgan edik).

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- O'zbekchada **"-ayotgan edi"** → past continuous; **"-di"** → past simple.
- **when** dan keyin odatda qisqa voqea (simple), **while** dan keyin uzun harakat (continuous).
- Vaqt chizig'ida tasavvur qiling: **nuqta** = simple, **chiziq** = continuous.

## ❌ Tipik xatolar
- ~~I didn't went~~ → I **didn't go** (did dan keyin 1-shakl)
- ~~When I was arriving, he left~~ → When I **arrived**, he **was leaving** / **left**
- ~~I was knowing~~ → I **knew** (state verb)
- ~~Did you saw?~~ → **Did** you **see**?

## ✅ O'zingni tekshir
- "Sen qo'ng'iroq qilganingda ovqat pishirayotgan edim" → I **was cooking** when you **called**.
- "Kecha ishga bormadim" → I **didn't go** to work yesterday.
- "Soat 9 da nima qilayotgan eding?" → What **were** you **doing** at 9?
