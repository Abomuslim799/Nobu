# Talaffuz: th /θ/ va /ð/
meta: Dushanba · 5 daq

## 🟢 Asos
O'zbek tilida yo'q tovush. Til uchini **tishlar orasiga** chiqaring va havo puflang.
| Tovush | Qanday | Misollar |
|---|---|---|
| /θ/ (jarangsiz) | faqat havo | **th**ink, **th**ree, ba**th**, mon**th** |
| /ð/ (jarangli) | havo + ovoz | **th**is, **th**at, mo**th**er, wi**th** |

> ⚠️ ~~sink~~ (think), ~~tree~~ (three), ~~zis~~ (this) — eng ko'p xato.

## 🟡 O'rta
Juftliklar bilan mashq (minimal pairs):
- think — sink — tink
- three — tree — free
- they — day — zay
- breathe /ð/ — breath /θ/

## 🔴 Advanced
- Tez nutqda: **in the** → /ɪnðə/, **at the** → /ətðə/ — til tishdan tishga sakraydi.
- Tongue twister: *The thirty-three thieves thought that they thrilled the throne throughout Thursday.*

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- Ko'zguda tekshiring: til uchi **ko'rinishi** kerak.
- Kuniga 10 marta "**I think that this is the thing**" — sekin, keyin tez.

## ✅ Bugungi topshiriq
- 5 juftlikni ovoz yozib oling, keyin modeldan eshiting va solishtiring.
