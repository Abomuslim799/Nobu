# Speaking: reja va bashorat
meta: Seshanba · future formalar · 10 daq

## 🟢 Asos
Uch qavat: **reja** (going to / present continuous), **bashorat** (will / might), **shart** (if + present).
EN: Next year I'**m going to** move to a new job. I **think** it **will** be hard at first, but if I **work** hard, I'**ll** manage.

Ochilishlar: **I'm planning to…**, **I'm hoping to…**, **I've decided to…**, **My goal is to…**

## 🟡 O'rta
- Ishonch darajasi: **definitely** → **probably** → **might** → **probably won't** → **definitely won't**.
- **By the end of the year I'll have…** — natijani ko'rsatish.
- **This time next year I'll be…** — kelajakdagi tasvir.

## 🔴 Advanced
- **I'm on the verge of…**, **I'm due to…**, **The plan is to…**
- Ehtimol: **There's a good chance that…**, **I doubt that…**, **Chances are…**
- Rejadan voz kechish: **I was going to…, but…**

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- Har javobda **reja + sabab + shubha** formulasi: I'm going to X **because** Y, **although** Z might happen.

## ✅ Bugungi topshiriq
- "Where do you see yourself in 5 years?" — 1.5 daqiqa.
- Kamida: going to, will have V3, will be -ing, 1 ta "chances are".
