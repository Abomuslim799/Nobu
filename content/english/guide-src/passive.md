# Passive — barcha zamonlarda
meta: Murphy EGU 42–44 · B1 → C1

## 🟢 Asos
**be + V3** — zamon "be" da o'zgaradi.
| Zamon | Faol | Majhul |
|---|---|---|
| Present simple | They **make** cars here. | Cars **are made** here. |
| Present continuous | They **are repairing** the road. | The road **is being repaired**. |
| Past simple | They **built** it in 1990. | It **was built** in 1990. |
| Present perfect | Someone **has stolen** my car. | My car **has been stolen**. |
| Future | They **will send** it. | It **will be sent**. |
| Modal | You **must do** it. | It **must be done**. |

## 🟡 O'rta
- **by + kim** — faqat muhim bo'lsa: The book was written **by Tolstoy**.
- Ikki to'ldiruvchili fe'llar (give, send, offer, tell, show): **I was given** a book (ko'proq tabiiy) / A book was given to me.
- Qachon majhul: ish qiluvchi noma'lum, muhim emas, yoki aniq (police arrested him → He **was arrested**).
- **get + V3** (gapirishda): He **got fired**. They **got married**.

## 🔴 Advanced
- Past perfect / future perfect passive: had been done, will have been done.
- Continuous passive: was being done (fon): The house **was being painted** when we arrived.
- Infinitive / gerund: I want **to be told**. I hate **being lied to**.
- Ilmiy va rasmiy uslubda majhul ko'p: *The samples were analysed…*

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- Majhulni tekshirish: zamonni faol gapda toping, keyin "be"ni o'sha zamonga qo'ying + V3.
- O'zbekcha "**-ildi, -indi, -ilgan**" → passive.

## ❌ Tipik xatolar
- ~~The road is repairing~~ → The road **is being repaired**
- ~~It was build~~ → It was **built**
- ~~I was borned~~ → I **was born**

## ✅ O'zingni tekshir
- "Mashinam o'g'irlandi" → My car **has been stolen**.
- "Yo'l hozir ta'mirlanyapti" → The road **is being repaired**.
