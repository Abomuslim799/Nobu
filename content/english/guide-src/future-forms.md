# Future: will / going to / present continuous
meta: Murphy EGU 19–23 · A2 → C1

## 🟢 Asos
| Shakl | Qachon | Misol |
|---|---|---|
| **will** | shu zahoti qaror, va'da, taklif, fikr/bashorat | I'**ll help** you. |
| **going to** | oldindan reja; ko'rinib turgan dalil | I'**m going to buy** a car. Look — it'**s going to rain**. |
| **present continuous** | kelishilgan uchrashuv | I'**m meeting** Ali at 6. |
| **present simple** | jadval, dastur | The train **leaves** at 7:30. |

## 🟡 O'rta
**when / if / as soon as / before / after / until** dan keyin — **present** (will emas):
EN: I'll call you **when I get** home. || ~~when I will get~~ emas
EN: **If it rains**, we'll stay in.

- **Shall I / we…?** — taklif: Shall I open the window? Shall we go?
- **will** fikr bilan: I **think** / I'**m sure** / **probably** + will: I don't think he'll come.
- **be about to** — hozir-hozir: The film **is about to** start.

## 🔴 Advanced
- **be to** (rasmiy, jadval/buyruq): The president **is to visit** Tashkent next week.
- **be due to**: The plane **is due to** land at 5.
- **be on the point of / be on the verge of + -ing**: The company **is on the verge of** collapsing.
- **future in the past**: I knew you **would** come. I **was going to** call, but…

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- Gapirayotgan payt qaror qildingizmi? → **will**. Oldin qaror qilganmisiz? → **going to**. Kimdir bilan kelishganmisiz (vaqt, joy)? → **continuous**.
- **when / if** dan keyin hech qachon will qo'ymang (vaqt va shart gaplarida).

## ❌ Tipik xatolar
- ~~When I will finish, I'll call~~ → When I **finish**, I'll call
- ~~I will meet my friend tomorrow at 6~~ (kelishilgan) → I'**m meeting**…
- ~~Will I open the window?~~ (taklif) → **Shall** I open the window?

## ✅ O'zingni tekshir
- "Telefon jiringlayapti — men olaman" → The phone's ringing — I'**ll get** it.
- "Ishni tugatishim bilan qo'ng'iroq qilaman" → I'll call you as soon as I **finish**.
