# Relative clauses
meta: Murphy EGU 92–97 · B1 → C1

## 🟢 Asos
| So'z | Kim/nima uchun | Misol |
|---|---|---|
| **who** | odam | The man **who** called is my boss. |
| **which** | narsa, hayvon | The book **which** I bought is good. |
| **that** | odam yoki narsa (norasmiy, defining) | The car **that** I want… |
| **whose** | egalik | The girl **whose** bag was stolen… |
| **where** | joy | The city **where** I was born… |
| **when** | vaqt | The day **when** we met… |

## 🟡 O'rta
**Defining** (aniqlovchi — vergulsiz, gapsiz ma'no tushunarsiz) vs **non-defining** (qo'shimcha ma'lumot — vergul bilan):
EN: My brother **who** lives in London is a doctor. || bir nechta akam bor — Londondagisi
EN: My brother, **who** lives in London, is a doctor. || bitta akam bor, qo'shimcha: Londonda yashaydi
- Non-defining'da **that ishlatilmaydi** va so'zni tushirib bo'lmaydi.
- Defining'da relativ so'z **ob'ekt** bo'lsa — tushirish mumkin: The film (**that**) I watched… 
- **which** butun gapga: He passed the exam, **which** surprised everyone.

## 🔴 Advanced
- Predlog + whom/which (rasmiy): the person **to whom** I spoke ↔ the person I spoke **to** (norasmiy).
- **some of / most of / none of + whom / which**: I have two brothers, **both of whom** are doctors.
- Qisqartirilgan (reduced) clauses: The man **standing** there… (= who is standing). The car **made** in Japan… (= which was made).
- **what** = the thing that: **What** I need is time. (~~The thing what~~)

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- Vergullarni olib tashlang — ma'no buziladimi? Buzilsa defining (vergulsiz), buzilmasa — non-defining.
- Gapirishda defining uchun doim **that** ishlatish xavfsiz (odam uchun ham).
- Relativ so'zdan keyin darhol ega kelsa (that **I** bought) — so'zni tushirish mumkin.

## ❌ Tipik xatolar
- ~~The man which called~~ → **who** called
- ~~My mother, that is a teacher,~~ → **who** is a teacher
- ~~The book that I bought it~~ → The book that I bought (ortiqcha "it" yo'q)
- ~~This is the thing what I need~~ → **what** I need / the thing **that** I need

## ✅ O'zingni tekshir
- "Mashinasi o'g'irlangan ayol politsiyaga qo'ng'iroq qildi" → The woman **whose** car was stolen called the police.
- "Menga kerak bo'lgan narsa — vaqt" → **What** I need is time.
