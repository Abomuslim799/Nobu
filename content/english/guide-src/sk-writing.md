# Writing: yozish
meta: Chorshanba, Shanba · 20 daq

## 🟢 Asos
Tuzilma: **kirish (fikr) → 2 ta abzats (sabab + misol) → xulosa**.
- Kirish: 1–2 gap, fikringiz aniq.
- Har abzats: **topic sentence** + tushuntirish + misol.
- Xulosa: fikrni boshqa so'zlar bilan takrorlang.

## 🟡 O'rta
- Bog'lovchilar: **However, Moreover, As a result, For instance, In contrast** (gapirishdagidan rasmiyroq).
- Gap uzunligini aralashtiring: qisqa + uzun.
- Turlar: email (rasmiy/norasmiy), fikr insho, xat, qisqa hisobot.

## 🔴 Advanced
- Hedging: *It could be argued that…*, *This suggests that…*
- Nominalization: *decide → decision*, *We analysed it* → *The analysis showed…*
- Inversion va cleft bilan urg'u: *Not only does it…*, *What matters most is…*

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- Yozib bo'lgach **AI chatda** tekshiring: "Mening xatolarimni ko'rsat, tuzatma" — avval o'zingiz tuzating.
- Xatolar — mistake bank'ga.
