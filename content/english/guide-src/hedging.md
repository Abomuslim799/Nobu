# Hedging + discourse markers
meta: AGU · B2 → C1

## 🟢 Asos
**Hedging** — fikrni yumshatish, 100% emas deb ko'rsatish. C1 nutqining eng muhim belgisi: qat'iy emas, ehtiyotkor, muloyim.
| To'g'ridan-to'g'ri | Yumshatilgan |
|---|---|
| This is wrong. | I'm **not sure** this is **quite** right. |
| You're late. | You **seem to be** a bit late. |
| It will fail. | It **might** not work as well as we hope. |

Asosiy so'zlar: **seem / appear / tend to**, **might / could / may**, **perhaps / possibly / probably**, **a bit / slightly / somewhat**, **I'd say / I suppose / it seems to me**.

## 🟡 O'rta
**Discourse markers** — gaplarni bog'lash:
| Vazifa | Gapirishda | Yozishda |
|---|---|---|
| qo'shish | also, plus, on top of that | moreover, furthermore, in addition |
| qarama-qarshilik | but, though, still | however, nevertheless, whereas |
| natija | so, that's why | therefore, consequently, as a result |
| misol | like, for example | for instance, such as |
| aniqlik | I mean, actually | in other words, namely |
| xulosa | anyway, so basically | in conclusion, overall |
| mavzu almashtirish | by the way, speaking of | as for, regarding |

## 🔴 Advanced
- Masofa qo'yish: **It is widely believed that…**, **There is some evidence to suggest…**, **To some extent…**, **Arguably,…**
- Rozi bo'lmaslik muloyim: **I see your point, but…**, **That's true to an extent, however…**, **I'm not entirely convinced that…**
- Vaqt yutish (filler) — tabiiy: **Well…**, **Let me think…**, **That's a good question…**, **How can I put it…**
- **would** bilan yumshatish: I **would** say… I **would** suggest… I **wouldn't** go that far.

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- Har bir qat'iy fikrga 1 ta hedge qo'shing: **I think**, **probably**, **a bit** — 3 ta so'z bilan nutq C1 ga yaqinlashadi.
- **actually** — "aslida" (tuzatish), ~~"hozir"~~ emas. "Hozir" = currently.
- Gapirishda **however** emas, **but / though** — tabiiyroq. However — yozishda.

## ❌ Tipik xatolar
- ~~Actually I live in Tashkent~~ (hozir ma'nosida) → **Currently** I live…
- ~~On the other hand~~ (birinchi tomon aytilmagan) → **However** / **But**
- ~~In my opinion I think~~ → In my opinion, … **yoki** I think…

## ✅ O'zingni tekshir
- "Bu reja ishlamaydi" ni yumshat → I'm **not sure** this plan **will** work.
- "Fikringizni tushunaman, lekin…" → I **see your point, but**…
