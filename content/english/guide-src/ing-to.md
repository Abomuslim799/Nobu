# -ing vs to
meta: Murphy EGU 53–66 · B1 → C1

## 🟢 Asos
Ba'zi fe'llardan keyin **-ing**, ba'zilaridan keyin **to + V1** keladi. Yodlash kerak — lekin guruhlab yodlash oson.
| + -ing | + to V1 |
|---|---|
| enjoy, finish, mind, avoid, suggest, consider, keep, practise, miss, imagine, deny, admit, give up, can't stand | want, need, decide, plan, hope, expect, agree, refuse, promise, manage, offer, learn, afford, seem, pretend |
EN: I **enjoy reading**. || ~~enjoy to read~~
EN: I **decided to stay**.

> 💡 Predlogdan keyin har doim **-ing**: interested **in learning**, good **at cooking**, before **leaving**, without **saying**.

## 🟡 O'rta
**Ikkalasi ham, ma'no o'zgaradi:**
| Fe'l | + -ing | + to V1 |
|---|---|---|
| remember | **qilganimni** eslayman: I remember **locking** the door. | **qilishni** unutmayman: Remember **to lock** the door. |
| forget | I'll never forget **meeting** you. | Don't forget **to call**. |
| stop | I stopped **smoking** (tashladim) | I stopped **to smoke** (chekish uchun to'xtadim) |
| try | try **opening** the window (sinab ko'r) | try **to open** (urinib ko'r — qiyin) |
| regret | I regret **saying** it (afsus) | I regret **to tell** you (rasmiy xabar) |

- **like / love / hate / prefer + -ing / to** — ma'no deyarli bir xil; **would like** → doim **to**: I'd like **to go**.
- **make / let + kim + V1** (to'siz): She **made me wait**. **Let me go**.
- **want / ask / tell + kim + to V1**: I want **you to help**.

## 🔴 Advanced
- **look forward to / be used to / object to / when it comes to + -ing** — "to" predlog: I look forward **to hearing** from you.
- Perfect / passive shakllar: I **regret having said** it. He **denied having been** there. I hate **being interrupted**.
- **see / hear + kim + V1** (to'liq voqea) / **-ing** (jarayon qismi): I saw him **cross** the road / **crossing** the road.
- **It's no use / There's no point / It's worth + -ing**: It's not worth **waiting**.

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- **-ing** ≈ "jarayon, oldin bo'lgan, umuman". **to** ≈ "maqsad, kelajak, keyin bo'ladigan".
- remember/forget/regret: **-ing = orqaga** qarash, **to = oldinga** qarash.
- Shubhada: o'rniga ot qo'yib ko'ring — "to" dan keyin ot tursa (look forward to **the party**), demak -ing.

## ❌ Tipik xatolar
- ~~I look forward to hear from you~~ → to **hearing**
- ~~She made me to wait~~ → made me **wait**
- ~~I suggest you to go~~ → I suggest (that) you **go** / I suggest **going**
- ~~I'm interested to learn~~ → interested **in learning**

## ✅ O'zingni tekshir
- "Eshikni yopishni unutma" → Don't forget **to close** the door.
- "Kutishning foydasi yo'q" → It's no use **waiting**.
- "Javobingizni kutib qolaman" → I look forward to **hearing** from you.
