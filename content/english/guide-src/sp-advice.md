# Speaking: maslahat va fikr
meta: Chorshanba · modallar · 10 daq

## 🟢 Asos
Maslahat zinapoyasi (yumshoqdan kuchliga):
| Yumshoq | O'rta | Kuchli |
|---|---|---|
| You **could** try… / **Maybe** you **could**… | You **should**… / **I'd** … | You **must**… / You'**d better**… |

Ochilishlar: **If I were you, I'd…**, **Have you thought about…?**, **Why don't you…?**, **It might be worth + -ing**

## 🟡 O'rta
- O'tmish tahlili: You **should have**… / You **could have**… / It **must have been** hard.
- Empatiya + maslahat: **I know it's tough, but…**, **I'd be careful about…**

## 🔴 Advanced
- Nozik tanqid: **It might not be the best idea to…**, **I'm not sure I'd…**
- Ehtimol + taxmin: He **can't have** known. She **might have** misunderstood.

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- Hech qachon **You must** dan boshlamang — qo'pol eshitiladi. **I'd** / **You could** — xavfsiz.

## ✅ Bugungi topshiriq
- Do'stingiz ishdan charchagan. 1 daqiqa maslahat bering.
- Kamida: 3 xil maslahat shakli, 1 ta should have V3.
