# must / have to / should
meta: Murphy EGU 31–35 · A2 → C1

## 🟢 Asos
| Shakl | Ma'no | Misol |
|---|---|---|
| **must** | o'zim zarur deb bilaman | I **must** call my mum. |
| **have to** | tashqi qoida, majburiyat | I **have to** wear a uniform. |
| **should / ought to** | maslahat | You **should** rest. |
| **mustn't** | taqiq | You **mustn't** smoke here. |
| **don't have to** | shart emas | You **don't have to** come. |

> ⚠️ **mustn't** ≠ **don't have to**. Mustn't — qilma! Don't have to — xohlasang qil, shart emas.

## 🟡 O'rta
- O'tmish va kelajakda must yo'q → **had to / will have to**: I **had to** wait.
- **had better** — aks holda yomon: You'**d better** hurry, or you'll miss the bus.
- **need to / don't need to / needn't**: You **needn't** bring anything.
- Savol: **Do I have to**…? (must'dan ko'ra tabiiyroq)

## 🔴 Advanced
- **should have V3** — afsus/tanqid: You **should have** told me.
- **needn't have V3** — qilding, lekin kerak emas edi: You **needn't have** bought flowers.
- **be supposed to** — kutilgan, qoida: You'**re supposed to** be at work!
- **should** taxmin: They **should** be home by now.

## ⚡ Hiylalar
- Kim talab qilyapti? **Men o'zim** → must. **Qoida/boshqalar** → have to.
- Gapirishda **have to** va **should** 90% holatda yetadi; must — kuchli his yoki yozma qoida.

## ❌ Tipik xatolar
- ~~You don't must~~ → You **mustn't** / **don't have to**
- ~~I must went~~ / ~~I musted~~ → I **had to** go
- ~~You should to rest~~ → You should **rest**

## ✅ O'zingni tekshir
- "Ertaga erta turishing shart emas" → You **don't have to** get up early tomorrow.
- "Shoshilganing ma'qul" → You'**d better** hurry.
